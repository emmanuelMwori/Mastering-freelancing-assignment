# PART 1: FREELANCING MINDSET

## 1.1 Mindset Shift

**Employee mindset I need to unlearn:**

Depending entirely on an employer to provide opportunities, assign responsibilities and determine my professional value.

**Freelancer mindset I am developing:**

As a freelancer, I must identify opportunities, market my skills, build professional relationships, solve clients' problems and charge competitive prices based on the value I provide.

## 1.2 Freelancing Myth

**Myth:** Freelancing is only suitable for people with many years of professional experience.

**Why this is false:**

Clients also consider practical skills, professionalism, communication, reliability, time management and problem-solving abilities.

Beginners can build credibility through sample projects, portfolios, practice assignments and consistent delivery of quality work.

## 1.3 My Three Marketable Skills

1. **Virtual Assistance and Administrative Support:** Managing emails, schedules, meetings, documents and administrative activities.
2. **Research and Report Writing:** Gathering information, organizing findings and preparing professional reports.
3. **Community Management and Coordination:** Managing online communities, coordinating activities, communicating with stakeholders and supporting events.

**My primary skill:** Virtual Assistance and Administrative Support.

I selected this skill because my experience in youth leadership and coordinating Young Africans Network has helped me develop communication, organization, administration and project coordination skills.

## 1.4 Lifestyle Boundaries

I will maintain a healthy work-life balance, avoid unrealistic deadlines and reserve Sundays for rest and personal development, except when urgent client commitments require attention.

## 1.5 Financial Goals

| Goal | Monthly Amount |
|---|---:|
| Minimum survival income | $200 |
| Target income | $400 |
| Stretch income | $2,000 |

- Minimum: Covers essential living expenses.
- Target: Covers expenses, savings and professional development.
- Stretch: Supports business growth, investments and possible team expansion.

**Financial discipline:** I intend to reserve 25% of freelance payments as a personal tax savings provision. This is a budgeting decision, not a statement of the statutory tax rate.
